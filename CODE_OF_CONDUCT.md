# Code of conduct

Onyx is a small project, and everyone who shows up here (in issues, discussions or anywhere else the project lives) should be able to do so without being treated badly.

## What's expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new or who don't speak English as a first language.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Explain why, and assume the other person means well.
- Keep feedback specific and useful. "This button is hard to find on small screens" helps; "this is bad" doesn't.
- Accept that the maintainer has the final say on what goes into Onyx.

## What isn't okay

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Discrimination based on who someone is: age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, trolling, or repeatedly derailing a conversation.

## What happens

Comments, issues or other content that break these rules can be edited, hidden or removed. People who keep breaking them can be blocked from the project.

## Reporting

If someone is making you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately through [github.com/Barbaqueda](https://github.com/Barbaqueda). Reports are kept confidential.
